[h1]Blades and Thrones[/h1]

[b]v0.4.0 · for EU5 1.4.0[/b]

When the guns roar and the banners rise, who truly rules the state — the throne, the merchants, or the men who hold the swords?

[b]Turn your army into a fourth political force.[/b] This expansion is built around the army-crown-estate triangle: your armies are no longer mere numbers on the map, but political actors with desires, loyalty, mutinies — and ambitions of their own. Keep your blade sharp... and beware it turning toward the throne.

[b]Core Features[/b]

• A new Soldiers estate — the army is politics: soldiers carry political weight, with 10 privileges (Soldier Registry, Professional Officer Corps, State Mercenary Contracts, Military Courts, Standing Army, Old Soldiers' Council, Idle Production, Farmer Supply Train, Royal Hand, Militia Autonomy)
• An independent pay system — decoupled from maintenance: arrears erode satisfaction and army loyalty; pay up to win them back
• Army loyalty & mutinies — visible and manageable: mutiny nights, loyalty petitions — suppress or placate?
• Military coups & juntas — after the army seizes power, choose between civilian restoration, institutionalization, or an iron purge
• The "Mercenary Mutiny" disaster — rely too much on hired swords and they may take over your country
• Landless army countries & exiles — defeat is not the end: wander, raid, lease out your army, settle new roots, or restore your old capital; keep playing as an army-state (five company types + the exile path)
• A military conversion panel — found or absorb companies, demobilize armies, resettle veterans, lease troops, grant settlement, claim a foothold, appoint company rulers, revoke or exchange warlord fiefs: nine conversions on one screen, with loyalty/crisis bars and hover explanations for every figure
• Political Influence integration (the 1.4 resource) — granting soldier privileges drains the crown's political capital, major military conversions spend it, and after a coup the settlement pays out as two lasting dividends: Recognition of Blood and Endorsement of the Army
• Military doctrine laws with era scaling — Standing Army / Mercenary League / Mixed / Levy, rising and fading across 1337-1836
• Soldier regencies, monthly intel reports, the fate of your veterans, and a new Militarism vs Civilianism societal axis
• Full AI support + an easter-egg landless state: the "Free Legion" (BLT)

[b]Design Philosophy[/b]
Power belongs to those who can defend it. Pay, loyalty, and political risk form the triangle: a loyal army is the pillar of the throne — or, if neglected, its gravedigger.
