一、军饷体系 Pay System
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
中文：
- 军饷公式：常备军每千人每月 5 金（regular_army_size × 0.005）+ 每座堡垒每月 1 金。
  与维护费完全脱钩（维护费只影响补给/满意度）。
- 欠饷：国库不足时欠饷月数 +1（上限 24 月）；每欠 1 月军队忠诚 -0.2%（满 24 月 = -4.8%）；
  补饷后每月 -1 回落。欠饷期间士兵阶层满意度弱惩罚。
- 额外军饷事件（忠诚低时）：支付 12 个月军饷 ×（威胁比率 1-3，封顶 3 倍）。

English:
- Pay formula: 5 gold per 1,000 regulars per month (regular_army_size × 0.005) +
  1 gold per fort per month. Fully decoupled from maintenance (maintenance only
  affects supply/satisfaction).
- Arrears: when the treasury is short, arrears +1/month (cap 24); each arrears
  month reduces army loyalty by 0.2% (24 months = -4.8%); repaying reduces 1/month.
  Soldier estate satisfaction takes a weak penalty while in arrears.
- Extra pay event (low loyalty): pay 12 months of pay × (threat ratio 1-3, cap 3x).

二、军队忠诚 Army Loyalty
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
中文：基础 0.5，各项加减：
- 士兵满意度：(满意度 - 0.5) × 0.3
- 士兵阶层力量：× 0.1
- 王室力量：× 0.2
- 陆军维护滑条：× 0.2；堡垒维护滑条：× 0.05
- 王室之手：满意度 ≥ 0.5 时 +0.1；< 0.5 时 -0.15（失控）
- 佣兵威胁（压力刻度 0-1）：× -0.25
- 战争荣耀（胜利事件变量）：× 0.1；战争耻辱（战败）：× -0.15
- 稳定度 < 0：- |稳定度| × 0.05
- 战争疲劳：- 疲劳 × 0.02
- 战时：+0.05
- 军制政策：常备军 +0.05；佣兵联盟 -0.05；混合 +0.02；征召 -0.05
- 欠饷：每欠 1 月 -0.002（见上）
- 范围 0-1

English: Base 0.5, then:
- Soldier satisfaction: (satisfaction - 0.5) × 0.3
- Soldier estate power: × 0.1
- Crown power: × 0.2
- Army maintenance slider: × 0.2; fort maintenance slider: × 0.05
- Royal Hand: +0.1 if satisfaction ≥ 0.5; -0.15 if < 0.5 (out of control)
- Mercenary pressure scale (0-1): × -0.25
- War glory (victory variable): × 0.1; war shame (defeat): × -0.15
- Stability < 0: - |stability| × 0.05
- War exhaustion: - exhaustion × 0.02
- At war: +0.05
- Military doctrine: standing +0.05; mercenary league -0.05; mixed +0.02; levy -0.05
- Arrears: -0.002 per arrears month (see above)
- Clamped 0-1

三、军事危机 Military Crisis (0-100)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
中文：月度变化 = 威胁比率档位 + 各项加成 + 消退：
- 威胁比率（佣兵总兵力 ÷ 有效可控军力）：
  ≥4 → +4.0 /  ≥3 → +3.0 /  ≥2 → +2.0 /  ≥1.5 → +1.5 /  ≥1 → +1.0
- 忠诚 < 0.35 → +0.5；忠诚 < 0.2 → 再 +0.5
- 士兵满意度 < 0.5 → +0.25
- 战时 → +0.25
- 消退：威胁 < 0.75 → -1.5/月；< 1 → -0.75/月
- 满 100 → 夺舍灾变必定触发（spawn +1.0）；另有小概率随机爆发。
- 危机化装叙述行：<30 / 30-60 / 60-90 / ≥90 四档（转化面板与情报汇总显示）。

English: Monthly change = threat tier + bonuses + decay:
- Threat ratio (mercenary strength ÷ effective loyal military):
  ≥4 → +4.0 / ≥3 → +3.0 / ≥2 → +2.0 / ≥1.5 → +1.5 / ≥1 → +1.0
- Loyalty < 0.35 → +0.5; loyalty < 0.2 → another +0.5
- Soldier satisfaction < 0.5 → +0.25
- At war → +0.25
- Decay: threat < 0.75 → -1.5/month; < 1 → -0.75/month
- At 100 → mutiny disaster guaranteed (spawn +1.0); small random chance otherwise.
- Flavor line: four tiers <30 / 30-60 / 60-90 / ≥90 (shown in conversion panel & intel).

四、夺舍灾变 Mutiny Disaster
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
中文：
- 开始条件：佣兵兵力 > 可控军力；无其他灾难；不在内战中；10 年冷却外。
- 月度触发概率：基础 0.1%；危机 100 → +100%（必触发）；
  威胁 >1.5 → +0.2%；>2 → +0.4%；>3 → +0.8%；
  满意度 <0.5 → +0.1%；<0.25 → +0.2%；≥0.75 → -0.1%。
- 灾变开始：危机清零；记录初始可控军力快照；生成叛军（初始进度 20%）。
- 叛军进度：每月按力量对比：
  佣兵 > 可控×1.25 → +0.15；> 可控 → +0.1；> 0.75× → 0；≤ 0.75× → -0.2
  满意度 <0.5 → +0.05；≥0.75 → -0.05
- 进度满 100 → 内战爆发（佣兵转叛军部队，叛军国家成立）。
- 进度归零 → 早期压制收场（无稳定奖励、危机残留 50）。
- 结算（按灾变开始时兵力 vs 可控军力快照）：
  > 125% → 夺舍（原国变流亡者）；75-125% → 无地军团国（不忠附庸）；
  < 75% 或叛军被镇压 → 剿灭（+稳定奖励，危机归零）。
- 结算后 10 年冷却。

English:
- Start: mercenary strength > loyal military; no other disaster; not in civil war;
  outside 10-year cooldown.
- Monthly spawn: base 0.1%; crisis 100 → +100% (guaranteed);
  threat >1.5 → +0.2%; >2 → +0.4%; >3 → +0.8%;
  satisfaction <0.5 → +0.1%; <0.25 → +0.2%; ≥0.75 → -0.1%.
- On start: crisis reset to 0; loyal military snapshot recorded; rebels spawn
  (initial progress 20%).
- Rebel progress per month by force comparison:
  mercs > loyal×1.25 → +0.15; > loyal → +0.1; > 0.75× → 0; ≤ 0.75× → -0.2
  satisfaction <0.5 → +0.05; ≥0.75 → -0.05
- Progress 100 → civil war (mercs become rebel units, rebel country formed).
- Progress 0 → early suppression ending (no stability reward, crisis left at 50).
- Settlement (by snapshot at start):
  > 125% → takeover (old country becomes exiles); 75-125% → territory-free
  company (disloyal subject); < 75% or rebels crushed → destroyed
  (+stability reward, crisis reset).
- 10-year cooldown after settlement.

十一、团练 Militia
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
中文：团练所是地方军事据点（本地士兵阶层力量 +5%、人力 +5%、征召规模 +10%），
代价是本地士兵食物消费 +10%；建造费 50 金（阶层承担）；

（SOLDIERS=40）吸引农民晋升填岗，形成"士兵阶层永动机"，永久抵消退伍安置/

不直接制造士兵 POP。
English: training ground is a local military post (local soldier estate power
+5%, manpower +5%, levy size +10%) at the cost of local soldier food
consumption +10%; build cost 50 gold (estate-funded); player: 20%/month
self-build; AI: 5%/month (needs satisfaction ≥ 0.5); revoking the privilege
stops new construction, existing grounds remain.
Design note: intentionally NO employment_size jobs - jobs attract farmers to
promote into soldiers at the highest weight (SOLDIERS=40), creating a
"soldier estate perpetual engine" that permanently cancels the demobilization
step of the core loop (resettlement / veterans return). So the training ground
only strengthens the source of troops, it does not manufacture soldier pops.
